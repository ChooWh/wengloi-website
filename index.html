Repair & Reconditioning

Worn shafts, gears, rollers, screw conveyors and rotors restored to spec — so you don't wait months for an overseas replacement.

⚙️

CNC Machining

CNC lathes and a 12,000 rpm high-speed machining centre for accurate, repeatable parts — one-off or batch production.

Manual Machining

Prototyping

Turn your drawing, sample or sketch into a working part. No drawing? We can measure and reverse-engineer from the worn original.

Modification

Welding & Fabrication

Build-up welding on worn surfaces before re-machining, plus general fabrication to complete the job in one place.

Since 1989

Built on 35+ years of keeping plants running

When a machine stops, everything stops. That's the problem Weng Loi Machinery has solved for plants, mills and contractors since 1989 — hundreds of clients served by consulting, designing and manufacturing optimised engineering parts, delivered by engineers.

Founded by Choo Tuon Loi, a machinist with over 50 years at the lathe, the company today runs a 19-person team that combines old-school machining craft with degree-qualified mechanical engineers and modern CNC capability.

We work across the full range of materials — steels, non-ferrous metals, plastics and composites — and we're investing ahead: 3D scanning, SLA/SLS 3D printing and multi-axis machining are on our roadmap.
