Weng Loi Machinery Engineering: frequently asked questions

What does Weng Loi Machinery Engineering do?

Weng Loi Machinery Engineering is a machine shop in Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia, operating since 1989. It offers repair and reconditioning of machinery parts, CNC machining, manual machining, prototyping, modification, and welding and fabrication, all under one roof.

Where is Weng Loi Machinery Engineering located?

The workshop is at No 5 & 7, Hala Menglembu Timur 1, Kawasan Perindustrian Ringan Menglembu Timur, 31450 Ipoh, Perak, Malaysia.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 7:30 pm, and Saturday and Sunday 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. The workshop is open on Sundays.

How long a shaft can Weng Loi turn?

Up to 4 metres (4,000 mm) in length and ø300 mm in diameter, on the long-bed lathes. Flanges and plates up to ø1,600 mm in diameter are faced on a separate face lathe. The workshop has 24 manual horizontal lathes in total.

What CNC capacity does Weng Loi have?

Three CNC lathes (up to ø440 mm diameter and 820 mm length, including high-precision machines) and one CNC machining centre with a 1,000 × 500 mm table and 700 kg maximum load.

What kinds of parts can Weng Loi repair or recondition?

Worn shafts, gears, rollers and rotors can be restored to specification, so customers do not have to wait months for an overseas replacement. Where needed, build-up welding is done on worn surfaces before re-machining.

Can Weng Loi make a part without a drawing?

Yes. Parts can be made from a drawing, a sample or a sketch. If there is no drawing, the team can measure and reverse-engineer the part from the worn original.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp a photo, drawing or sample of the part to Lily on +60 17-701 4268. A phone photo with rough dimensions is enough to start. The engineers assess whether to repair, recondition or make a new part, and quote the practical, economical option. For urgent breakdowns, say so in the message.

What materials does Weng Loi machine?

Steels, non-ferrous metals, plastics and composites.

How long has Weng Loi been in business, and who founded it?

Weng Loi has operated in Ipoh since 1989. It was founded by Choo Tuon Loi, a machinist with over 50 years at the lathe. The company today has a team of 19.
